# Candidate outreach drafting

For each person a recruiter or founder has already chosen to approach about an approved role: a first message and two follow-ups, each personal line traced to a public work source the recruiter supplied, do-not-contact names and opt-outs honoured, and a reply log. The recruiter sends every message.

*Workflow template · Recruiting and hiring · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/candidate-outreach-drafting/)*

## The outcome

Every person you have decided to write to gets a message that reads as if you wrote it for them, because the one personal line in it comes from something they did in their public work, which you found and named. Two follow-ups are ready behind it, dated in your working days, each adding one new fact about the role so nobody receives the same message twice.

Nobody on your do-not-contact list is written to, and nobody who has answered, declined or opted out gets another message. A detail from someone's private life, or anything that touches who they are rather than what they do, is left out and the reason noted. The pack ends with a reply log: where each person stands and what happens next.

It drafts and nothing more. It never finds anyone, never judges anyone against the role and never puts your list in any order but yours. It says nothing about pay or terms beyond what your role brief allows. You read every message and send it yourself.

## Who starts the work, and when

The recruiter, or the founder or hiring manager doing their own hiring, starts it once the role is approved and they have chosen the people to approach, before any message goes out. Run it again each time replies come in or a follow-up falls due, with the reply log, so every answer stops the messages to that person.

## What they supply

- The approved role brief, saying which of the pay range, location and hiring steps may appear in a message.
- Your company facts page: what the company does, what may be said about the team, what is never said, who signs, the ask and your opt-out line.
- The approach list: for each person, the name to greet them by, how you will reach them, and one thing they did in public work with where and when it appeared, plus who referred them if that person agreed to be named.
- Your do-not-contact list.
- From the second run on, the reply log so far.

## What comes out

One pack in a fixed order: the checks made before drafting; who was not drafted and why; for each person, in your order, the three dated messages and where the personal line came from; the details left out and why; the reply log; anyone to add to your do-not-contact list; what ends the messages; what to read before sending; and the defaults used. Every personal line can be traced to a row you supplied.

See the worked example with invented data in [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md).

## What has to change for your company

Write your facts page once and keep it current; the messages say nothing else about the company. Settle your opt-out line with your people lead or your advisers for the places you write to, and name who keeps the do-not-contact list and how an opt-out reaches it the same day. Decide what each role brief allows in a message, such as whether the pay range goes in a first approach. Set your working days, the gaps between messages, the word limits, how old a work detail may be and how long to wait after a "not now". Agree where the reply log lives and who updates it after each send.

It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the files you supply, with no connection to any mailbox, professional network or recruiting system.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
