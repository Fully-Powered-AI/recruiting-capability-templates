# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One openly licensed package informed it: the talent-sourcer package in rade-nugroho/Agentic-Enteprises-Skill (MIT), mainly its reference on outreach and engagement, with its notes on pipeline stages. From the outreach reference come opening with a single detail from the person's public work; short messages that are truthful about the role and the company and ask for something small, such as a brief call; a limited number of messages spaced in working days, each bringing something new rather than a bare reminder; a message that names the person who made a referral; coming back months later to someone for whom the timing was wrong; and an opt-out line, keeping to every unsubscribe and the do-not-contact list, no return to people who said no, and personal matters kept out of messages. The package's overview and its scope note state two of those rules as well: personalize from public work without inference, and stop at once on an unsubscribe or a decline. From the pipeline notes comes following each person from first contact through a reply to a later revisit or a close, the root of the reply log. Fully Powered AI added the rule that each personal line traces to a source the recruiter supplied, public, about work and within a freshness limit; the full list of characteristics never mentioned or inferred; the exact and possible-match check against the do-not-contact list; pay and terms only as the role brief allows; a referrer named only if they agreed; drafting in the recruiter's own order; the reply log's events, statuses and next actions; the dating rules; the word limits; and the output format, stop conditions, tests and example. The package's search, talent-mapping, fit-rating, handoff and metrics material is left out, and no one is found, assessed or sorted. No third-party text is reused.
