# Candidate outreach drafting: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the role, the people, the addresses, the blogs, meetups and conferences, and every word of the notes below are invented. Company A stands for an invented 30-person company that makes route-planning software for regional delivery firms. Blog B, Meetup C and the open-source library mentioned are invented too, and every address uses a reserved example domain. Sam R., the co-founder and CEO, is hiring a senior data engineer and has already chosen five people to write to.

The inputs come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. The pack the capability returned follows.

## How the dates and checks were worked out

- **Do-not-contact.** P3's contact, ren.t@example.net, matches an entry exactly, so nothing is drafted and no reason is given. No other contact or full name on the approach list matches an entry.
- **Reply log.** P5's latest event is `not now` on 4 November 2026 with the date 2027-03-31, so P5 is paused until 31 March 2027 and nothing is drafted. The earlier first message is shown under Sent.
- **Details.** P1 (June 2026) and P4 (August 2026) are public, about work and within 24 months of 10 November 2026, so each has a personal line. P2's detail is from a personal account and not about work, so it is not used. P3's and P5's details are not needed, as neither is drafted.
- **Follow-up 1.** Counting working days from Thursday 12 November: Friday 13 (1), Monday 16 (2), Tuesday 17 (3), Wednesday 18 (4).
- **Follow-up 2.** Six working days after Wednesday 18 November: Thursday 19 (1), Friday 20 (2), Monday 23 (3), Tuesday 24 (4), Wednesday 25 (5), then Thursday 26 and Friday 27 are non-working dates and the weekend follows, so Monday 30 (6).
- **What each follow-up adds.** The brief allows the pay range, location and pattern, so follow-up 1 carries them, the first item on the list. Follow-up 2 carries the hiring steps, the next item. Because the brief allows both, the facts-page line about the team is not reached and is not used. The first messages carry none of the allowed items, which are kept for the follow-ups.
- **Word counts,** from the line after the greeting to the line before the sign-off: P1's first message 95 words, P2's 72, P4's 100, P4's counting the referrer line; each follow-up 1 is 51 words and each follow-up 2 is 49, all within the limits of 120 and 80.
