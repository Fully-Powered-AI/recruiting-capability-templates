# Work sample exercise brief

For one approved role, the hiring manager gets a practical exercise: a bounded task from the role's real work, instructions for the candidate with a time limit and adjustments, and the evidence the panel will look for. The panel judges the work; nothing is rated here.

*Workflow template · Recruiting and hiring · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/work-sample-exercise-brief/)*

## The outcome

Every candidate for the role does the same small piece of the real work, with the same materials, the same time and the same help. The hiring manager knows why that task was chosen, which of the agreed criteria it can show and which it cannot, and what the panel will point to in a finished piece of work. Candidates know before they start how long it should take, what they will hand in, what the panel looks at and what the company will not do with their work.

The exercise is built on invented materials, never a live client's papers or a former employer's, and never on work the company would use. The time limit sits inside the hiring manager's approved effort. Adjustments are offered to everyone in the same words, so no one has to explain why they need one.

The brief ends with a blank record sheet for each panel member. It has no total, no weighting and no scale. The template prepares the exercise; it never reads a submission, and it never rates, ranks or compares anyone.

## Who starts the work, and when

The hiring manager starts it once the role description and the criteria are approved and the interview plan exists or is under way, and before any candidate is given an exercise. The people lead supplies the time limit, the adjustments the company has already agreed, the payment rule and how long submissions are kept. The hiring manager reads the draft, approves the task and the effort, and decides what is shared. Run it again when the criteria or the role change.

## What they supply

- The approved role description, and the approved criteria with what clear evidence looks like for each.
- A list of the role's real tasks, with how often each is done, roughly how long it takes and what information it uses.
- The settings: the longest time a candidate may spend, where and when the exercise is done, the adjustments already agreed, the payment rule, how long submissions are kept, who is on the panel and who the candidate may contact.
- The company's standing wording for the adjustments question and for what happens next.
- The hiring manager's own notes on the exercise, if any.

## What comes out

One brief in a fixed order: why this task, the exercise and its materials, the time and effort, the accessibility arrangements, the instructions written for the candidate, the evidence the panel will look for, a blank record sheet, how the submissions are handled, and review notes for the hiring manager.

The review notes list what was used, what was changed, what was left out and why, what was declined, and the open questions for each person. Missing wording is a marked gap, never a guess. Nothing has been sent to a candidate, and the brief says so on its last line.

## What has to change for your company

Set the longest time a candidate may spend, and the rule for paying candidates when an exercise runs longer. Decide whether submissions are reviewed with names removed, how long they are kept and who may see them. List the adjustments you have already agreed, and write the sentence every candidate is asked. Supply the wording about what happens next. Decide who sits on the panel and who approves the brief.

What the company owes a candidate who asks for an adjustment is a question for your people lead and advisers; the template applies the arrangements you list and does not judge them. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the documents you supply, and it needs no connection to your applicant tracking system.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
