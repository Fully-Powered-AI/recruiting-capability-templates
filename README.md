# Fully Powered Capability Templates: Recruiting and Hiring

Hire like a company ten times your size. These capability templates take the preparation around a hire and give an AI assistant a precise way to do it. They never score, rank or screen a candidate: people make every hiring decision.

This repository holds 5 capability templates for hiring managers, recruiters and people teams. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The capabilities

- **[Candidate outreach drafting](candidate-outreach-drafting/)**: For each person a recruiter or founder has already chosen to approach about an approved role: a first message and two follow-ups, each personal line traced to a public work source the recruiter supplied, do-not-contact names and opt-outs honoured, and a reply log. The recruiter sends every message.
- **[Interview plan and scorecard](interview-plan-and-scorecard/)**: Before the first interview, the hiring manager and every interviewer share one plan: the criteria the role needs, the same questions in the same order for every candidate, and a blank scorecard each person fills in alone. It rates no one. People make every decision about a candidate.
- **[Interview scheduling and candidate confirmation](interview-scheduling-and-candidate-confirmation/)**: For candidates your team has already chosen to interview, the recruiting coordinator gets one scheduling sheet that lines up each candidate's offered times, in their own time zone, with the interviewers' free time, plus a ready confirmation or availability request for each. A person sends every message and invitation.
- **[Job description](job-description/)**: A hiring manager’s rough notes become a job description the right people want to answer: why the role exists, the work itself, and only the must-haves that are real. Your approved pay and employer wording go in untouched, and nothing is posted until the hiring manager and the people lead approve it.
- **[Job posting and sourcing plan](job-posting-and-sourcing-plan/)**: For one approved role, the hiring manager gets a plan for where to post and where to look: every channel on the company's own list ordered by the signal it should give for that kind of role, a week-by-week schedule inside the budget, and what to count on each channel. Nothing is posted or paid until a person approves.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
