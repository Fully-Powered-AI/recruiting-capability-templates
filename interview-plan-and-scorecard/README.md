# Interview plan and scorecard

Before the first interview, the hiring manager and every interviewer share one plan: the criteria the role needs, the same questions in the same order for every candidate, and a blank scorecard each person fills in alone. It rates no one. People make every decision about a candidate.

*Workflow template · Recruiting and hiring · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/interview-plan-and-scorecard/)*

## The outcome

Every candidate meets the same interview. The criteria come straight from the job description you already approved, each stage knows which of them it covers, and the questions ask about work people have actually done, so the people deciding can judge each answer on evidence instead of on who interviewed best.

The plan also takes out what should never be in an interview. A question about family, a wish for someone younger: each is removed and flagged for your people lead, with nothing put in its place. A vague call for "culture fit" is removed too, and the hiring manager is asked which behaviour was meant. That is how a growing company interviews like one with a full recruiting team.

The plan is a structure and nothing more. It never reads a CV, never sees a candidate, and never rates, ranks or compares anyone. Interviewers write down what they heard before choosing a rating, the form has no total, and the hiring manager decides.

## Who starts the work, and when

The hiring manager starts it once the role and its description are approved, before the first interview is booked. The people lead supplies the stages, the interviewers and the topics your company never raises in an interview. Both read the draft and approve it, and then a person shares it with the interviewers. Run it again whenever the description, a stage or an interviewer changes.

## What they supply

- The approved job description or role brief: what success looks like, the must-haves and the ways of working.
- The hiring manager's notes for the interviews, if any: favourite questions, an exercise they want, who should be involved.
- From the people lead: the stages, how long each lasts and who runs it, who decides, and the words every candidate hears about next steps.
- The people lead's list of topics never raised in an interview.
- How many questions each criterion should get, and whether there is a work exercise with a second attempt after feedback.

## What comes out

One interview plan in a fixed order: what the role must achieve, the criteria with what clear evidence looks like for each, the stages, the questions for each stage with follow-ups, the work exercise, a coverage check, a blank scorecard for each stage, and how the debrief runs.

Review notes come with it for the two approvers. They list every question that was rewritten, every item removed and why, every request that was declined, such as ranking finalists or letting one candidate skip a stage, and the open questions for each person. Missing wording is a marked gap, never a guess. The plan ends with the line that nothing has been shared and no candidate has been rated, ranked or compared.

## What has to change for your company

Set your stages, their lengths and who runs each. Choose how many questions each criterion gets and whether roles include a work exercise. Keep or replace the rating scale, but keep the rule that evidence is written before the rating. Supply the wording every candidate hears: the question about adjustments, the next steps, anything about hours.

The list of topics never raised is your people lead's, kept current with your advisers; what may be asked in an interview differs from place to place, and the template applies your list without judging it. Decide who approves the plan, who decides about each candidate, and where completed forms are kept. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the documents you supply, and it needs no connection to your applicant tracking system.

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
