# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One openly licensed method informed it: the hiring scorecard in swan-gtm/gtm-skills (MIT). From it come starting from the results the role must produce and drawing the criteria from them; giving each interview stage its own criteria, decided in advance; asking only about what candidates have done, never what they would do, with at least two questions for each criterion by default; one question about something the candidate owned that went wrong; a work exercise in which the candidate gets feedback and tries again, and knows beforehand that this will happen; behaviours an interviewer can observe in place of "culture fit"; each interviewer writing a rating independently and handing it in before the debrief, with the least senior heard first and disagreements taken first; the hiring manager deciding, with the debrief informing the decision rather than voting on it; naming what is still unknown about a candidate; the levels of the default rating scale, which follow the source's evidence standard (a specific example and its result, a smaller or one-off example, nothing specific); and a referred candidate going through the same process as everyone else. Fully Powered AI's own are the rule that the plan prepares structure only and rates, ranks and compares no one; the scorecard with no total, no weighting and evidence written before the rating; criteria taken only from the approved role description; the topics-never-raised check; the coverage check; the review notes, stop conditions, tests and worked example. The source's weighted scores, decision package, reference checks and questions about personal goals are left out. No third-party text is reused.
