# Job description: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Tallowmere Fabrication, its people, its figures and every word of its notes and employer wording below are invented. It is an invented 85-person metal fabrication shop that has never had a production scheduler. Its operations manager has approval to hire one and typed some notes between shifts.

The inputs come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. The job description and its review notes follow.

## The inputs

### 1. The hiring manager's notes

Typed by the operations manager, as supplied.

```
Need a production scheduler. Right now I build the weekly schedule myself
on Sunday nights in a spreadsheet and it's wrong by Tuesday. 3 lines -
laser, press brake, weld - 2 shifts. Late jobs went up since we took on
the trailer-parts customer in spring. On-time delivery was 81% last
quarter, we promise customers 95.

Person would own the weekly + daily schedule, run the 7:15 production
meeting, chase material shortages with purchasing, tell customer service
early when a job will slip, keep routings and run times in the ERP up to
date.

Want: rockstar scheduler, 10+ yrs manufacturing, bachelor's degree,
5 years on our ERP system, Excel wizard, young and energetic, must be a
culture fit, thick skin, forklift license, lean six sigma black belt, can
read drawings, must handle fast-paced environment. Good communicator.
Someone like Dennis who retired.

Success: by 3 months they build the schedule without me and the Sunday
spreadsheet is dead. By a year on-time delivery is 95 and overtime is
down.

Reports to me. Works with 2 shift supervisors, purchasing lead, customer
service (3). First scheduler we've had.
```
