# Job posting and sourcing plan: a trial on the invented example

- **Product:** Claude, run in Claude Code
- **Model:** Claude Opus 5.5 (model ID claude-opus-5-5)
- **Date:** 2026-11-10

The inputs for this run are the invented example in `public/example.md` (the role brief, the channel list, the channel record, and the budget, time and settings). No client data was used.

This record replaces the record of an earlier run on the same day, by a different model, which reached the same figures, ranks, weeks and decisions as the published example and listed several places where the instructions were ambiguous. The template was then edited to settle most of them: a channel moved later by the weekly cap is now marked as such; the cap check now counts the channels already in the plan and later monthly charges; a channel that starts in the review week is no longer listed at the review for spend without a first interview; only fees of channels in the plan are listed as payable on a hire; and the instruction block now carries the rules for missing or unusual inputs that were previously only in the body of the template. The example's output was brought into line with those edits.

This run was made after those edits, by the assistant that made them. It is not blind: the assistant had read the published example output and the earlier run's record before running the template. It followed the instruction block and output format on the example's inputs and worked every figure again from the inputs, and only then compared its output with the published example.

## What was observed

- **Every required part was produced.** All seven sections and the closing line, in the required order. Nothing in the inputs called for a stop: the brief says the role is approved, the headers match, and the budget, weeks and week-1 date are given.
- **Difference from the published example: none.** The output matches the example's output line for line. That is expected and proves little, because this run was not blind: the assistant had read the example before running the template.
- **The edits took effect on this example.** Trade association D would take week 1 to $430, over the $400 cap; started in week 2 it is charged in weeks 2 and 6, neither week goes over the cap, and it is marked "moved by the weekly cap". Because it starts in the review week, the review line names it as started in week 2 rather than listing it for spend without a first interview. Agency E is not in the plan, so its fee appears only in section 2.
- **Figures worked again from the inputs.** 4 of 14 is 28.6% (High); 3 of 64 is 4.7% (Low in place of the default Medium); 1 of 6 and 3 of 5 are below the minimum of 10. 28 days from Monday 16 November, counted inclusively, ends on Sunday 13 December, in week 4. The review week ends on Sunday 29 November. Spend $180 + $250 + $250 = $680, leaving $820. Hours 2.5 in week 1, 3 in weeks 2 to 4, 2 in weeks 5 and 6.
- **Facts not in the inputs.** None was added. The "careers office" wording comes from the channel list's notes. The example's introduction (a 40-person workshop, applicants wanted before the holidays) is not in the inputs and was not used.
- **Ambiguities that remain:**
  - The family choice is a judgement. A "Workshop team lead" is a trade role with leading duties; Senior leadership could be argued. The template handles this only by marking it "to confirm" with an open question.
  - A month is counted as four weeks. The template now says so under What to customize and asks the budget holder to check a calendar-month provider's billing dates, but the plan itself still places the charge on the fourth week.
  - Reach asks for channels whose notes say they reach groups the company wants to reach more of. College C's note says the careers office shares it with final-year students and recent leavers, which is not the same thing, so "none marked in the channel list" was written; the judgement is still the reader's.
  - The exception at the review covers a channel that started in the review week after week 1. A channel moved by the cap to a week before the review week (possible when the review week is later than week 2) is listed as usual, even if it has run only a short time.
- **Not reached by this run.** The stop conditions, the declines, a blank hours column, a missing weekly cap or pay range, a record row for a channel not on the list, the reach addition, and an agency's percentage fee worked out on both ends of the pay range for an agency in the plan. The acceptance tests that would reach them are proposed and have not been run.
- **Shortcomings.** This is one run, by one model, on one invented example, not blind to the published output. It shows that the edited instructions can be followed to the published result and says nothing about other inputs or other products. A blind run by an assistant that has not seen the example is still to do.
