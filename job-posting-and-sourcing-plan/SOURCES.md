# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One openly licensed method informed it: the job posting strategy in LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT), in its job-posting-strategy package under skills/founder-led. From it come choosing channels by the kind of role and putting them in order of how much useful response they bring for what they cost; using a few well-chosen channels rather than every board; looking for people through the team's and investors' networks and through communities as well as by posting; counting the quality of applicants by channel so the mix can be changed; and posting to channels that reach groups the company wants more applicants from, which the source lists as diversity-focused channels. The source's channel lists are written for software companies and name particular boards, communities and agencies. Fully Powered AI replaced them with nine channel types and its own starting table for six role families, and added the rule that only the company's own channel list and prices are used, the company's own counts replacing the defaults, the rules for choosing within the budget, the team's hours and a weekly cap, the weekly schedule and its arithmetic, posts that run out mid-search, fees payable only on a hire, the measurement and review rules, the check that at least one channel is open to anyone, and the rule that no candidate is seen or assessed. The source's posting template, wording advice, outreach messages and reference checks are left out. No third-party text is reused.
