# Job posting and sourcing plan

For one approved role, the hiring manager gets a plan for where to post and where to look: every channel on the company's own list ordered by the signal it should give for that kind of role, a week-by-week schedule inside the budget, and what to count on each channel. Nothing is posted or paid until a person approves.

*Capability template · Recruiting and hiring · version 0.1.0 · [Capabilities Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/capabilities/job-posting-and-sourcing-plan/)*

## The outcome

The role goes out to the few places most likely to bring people who can do the work, instead of to every board at once. Each channel you use gets an expected signal for this kind of role: from your own counts where you keep them, and from a starting table by role family where you do not. The plan takes channels in that order until the budget, the team's hours or your weekly spending limit says stop, and it shows what each week costs, what is left, and which paid posts run out before the search ends.

Every channel in the plan also gets its count: what arrives, how it is tagged, who counts it, and a review after the second week at which the hiring manager decides what carries on. Search by search, the plan moves off the starting table and onto your own numbers.

It prepares and nothing more. It names no board and no price you did not give it, it posts and pays for nothing, and it never sees an applicant. People make every decision about who goes forward.

## Who starts the work, and when

The hiring manager starts it, or the recruiter or office manager working for them, once the role is approved and its description agreed and before anything is posted. The budget holder approves the spend. Run it again at the review point with the counts so far, or whenever the budget, the dates or your list of channels change.

## What they supply

- The approved role brief: title, team, location and hours, pay range, openings and must-haves.
- Your channel list: each board, community, college, referral scheme, agency or service you are willing to use, with its cost, how it is charged, the team time it takes each week and who looks after it.
- Your counts from earlier searches, if you keep them: applicants and first interviews for each channel.
- The budget, any weekly limit, the length of the search and the team hours available each week.

## What comes out

One plan in a fixed order: the role in brief; every channel in order, with the reason for its place and whether it made the plan; a weekly schedule with the spend, running total and hours; what to count on each channel and the rules for the review; a check that at least one channel is open to anyone; numbered decisions for the hiring manager and the budget holder; and the defaults used. Fees paid only when someone is hired, such as a referral bonus or an agency fee, sit apart from the budget. Every figure can be checked by hand.

See the worked example with invented data in [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md).

## What has to change for your company

Write your channel list once, with real costs and owners, and keep it current; the plan uses nothing else. Choose how many channels run at once, which week the review falls in, and how many applicants a channel needs before its own counts replace the starting table. Move channel types up or down the table for your market. Decide what counts as a first interview and where the counts are kept, and name who approves the plan and who holds the budget.

Any rule about where a role must be advertised, or what an advertisement must say, comes from your people lead and your advisers; the template applies none of its own. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the documents you supply, with no connection to any job board or applicant tracking system.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
