# Job posting and sourcing plan: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, its roles, its channels, its costs, its counts and every word of its notes below are invented. Trade Board A, General Board B, College C, Trade association D, Agency E and Profile search service F stand for channels a company might use; none is a real organisation. The company is an invented 40-person bench joinery workshop that makes fitted furniture. Its operations manager has approval to hire a workshop team lead and wants applicants in front of them before the holidays.

The inputs come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. The plan the workflow template returned follows.

## How the figures were worked out

- **Signal from the record.** Trade Board A: 4 of 14 is 28.6%, at or above 20%, so High, the same as the family default. General Board B: 3 of 64 is 4.7%, below 8%, so Low in place of the default Medium. Staff referral ask (5 applicants) and College C (6) are below the minimum of 10, so each keeps its default.
- **Order within a level.** In High, Trade Board A comes first because its record counted; College C (no spend) comes before the staff referral ask, which is paid per hire and goes last. In Medium, the careers page (no spend) comes before the bulletin ($250 in weeks 1 and 5 if started in week 1: $500), and Agency E, a percentage fee, goes last. In Low, General Board B's record counted, so it comes before Profile search service F.
- **Choosing.** The first four enter in week 1, using 2.5 of the 6 hours and $180. The bulletin would take week 1 to $430, over the $400 cap, so it starts in week 2, marked as moved by the weekly cap, charged in weeks 2 and 6 (neither week then goes over $400), and uses 0.5 more hours: five channels, the limit. Because it starts in week 2, the review after week 2 names it as started that week rather than listing it for spend without a first interview. Agency E stops at the channel limit. General Board B and Profile search service F are Low with five High or Medium channels already in, so they stop at signal.
- **Spend.** $180 + $250 + $250 = $680; $1,500 − $680 = $820 unspent. No week goes over $400.
- **The post's last day.** 28 days from Monday 16 November, counted inclusively, ends on Sunday 13 December, in week 4.
- **Hours.** Weeks 2 to 4: 1 + 0.5 + 0.5 + 0.5 + 0.5 = 3. Weeks 5 and 6, without Trade Board A: 2.
