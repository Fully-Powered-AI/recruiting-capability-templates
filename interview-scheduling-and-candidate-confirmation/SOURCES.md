# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One openly licensed method informed it: the candidate availability call in CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents (MIT), read in three of its files under skills/candidate-availability-call: the main instructions, the safety notes and the input checker. That package has an AI caller phone a candidate about interview times. From it come keeping contact with a candidate to timing alone, with no interviewing, assessing, talk of pay or promises; offering a candidate only times the company can really meet; settling the candidate's time zone and any limits on their days; taking no time as suitable until the candidate has said so, and passing anything unclear to a person; leaving the final time, the invitation and any change to the tracking system to a person; the subjects never raised with a candidate, which this template spells out in full; never presenting a time as fixed before a person has sent the invitation; no pressing a candidate and no repeat contact unless a person decides; keeping only what scheduling needs; and checking that the inputs are complete, and that the request's own wording is free of sensitive subjects, before anything is prepared. Fully Powered AI replaced the phone call with written drafts a person sends, and added the scheduling sheet: windows turned into one planning time zone day by day, matched against the time every required interviewer is free, start-time steps and buffers, an order that turns only on how many times each candidate offered, alternatives not held, no time offered twice, each interviewer's load, the rules for reading loose replies, reasons for times never repeated, unread columns listed, the review notes, the tests and the worked example. The source's call script, voicemail, phone-number handling and consent to follow-up channels are left out. No third-party text is reused.
