Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One openly licensed package informed it: the talent acquisition package in rade-nugroho/Agentic-Enteprises-Skill (MIT), listed with its commit and paths in this template's sources. From its notes on the applicant-tracking pipeline come a time limit for each stage, a role counted as ageing when it has been open a long while with few people at the later stages, and a weekly look at counts by stage and at the blockers holding a role up, such as interviewers who cannot be booked. From its notes on recruiting metrics come measuring pass-through step by step, from the people who reached a stage to those who went on from it, judging a source by how many of its applicants reach an interview, the option of leaving time on hold out of the days a role has been open where the system records it, and a short report that ends in actions rather than a wall of figures. Fully Powered AI's own are the fixed export columns with a stop when a header does not match; pass-through that leaves out people still waiting at a stage; the minimum before a rate is shown; counts only, with names, notes and personal details never read; blockers carried forward until someone marks them settled, those with no owner first; the owner of each wait taken from the stage owners you set; roles on hold shown but not flagged; and the stop conditions, tests and worked example. No third-party text is reused.
